# Vision

The Secret Family is a spatial web app in the Sci-Fi Labs catalog: a hierarchy you walk in the browser.

It is a visualization of claims from a public interview, not an endorsement of those claims. It belongs in the public layer — websites you publish yourself — alongside Where is Paul?, Musical Cubes, and Neuro Graph.

The private layer (Knowledge Graph, air-gapped drive) and the OS layer (Omarchy) are separate. This repo stays a static graph anyone can open without an account.
